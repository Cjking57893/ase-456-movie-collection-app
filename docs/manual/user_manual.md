## Introduction

Welcome to the **Movie Collection App**! This application helps you organize, track, and manage your personal movie collection. Whether you collect physical media (DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K UHD) or simply want to keep track of movies you own or want to watch, this app makes it easy.

### Key Features

✅ **Organize Your Collection** - Keep track of all your movies in one place  
✅ **Wishlist Management** - Save movies you want to watch or buy later  
✅ **Movie Search** - Find and import movie information from online databases  
✅ **Statistics** - View insights about your collection  
✅ **Cloud Sync** - Access your collection from any device  
✅ **Dark Mode** - Comfortable viewing in any lighting condition

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. **Launch the App**
   - Open the Movie Collection App on your device

2. **Sign Up**
   - On the login screen, tap **"Don't have an account? Sign Up"**
   - Enter your email address
   - Create a secure password (minimum 6 characters)
   - Tap **"Sign Up"** to create your account

3. **Verify Your Email** (if required)
   - Check your email inbox for a verification message
   - Click the verification link (if applicable)

### First Login

1. Enter your email address
2. Enter your password
3. Tap **"Sign In"**

> **Tip:** Your data is securely stored in the cloud, so you can access your collection from any device by logging in with your account.

## Managing Your Movie Collection

### Viewing Your Movies

1. After logging in, you'll see your **Movie List** page
2. All your added movies are displayed here
3. Scroll through the list to browse your collection

### Adding a Movie Manually

1. From the Movie List page, tap the **"+" (Add)** button
2. Fill in the movie details:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Director**
   - **Description**
   - **Release Year**
   - **Runtime** (in minutes)
   - **Rating** (e.g., PG-13, R)
   - **Personal Rating** (your own rating)
   - **Format** (DVD, Blu-ray, 4K UHD, Digital, VHS, Other)
   - **Storage Location** (Living Room, Bedroom, Storage, Office, Other)
   - **Genres** (Action, Comedy, Drama, etc.)
   - **Actors**
   - **Watched Status** (check if you've watched it)
   - **Notes** (personal notes about the movie)

3. Tap **"Add Movie"** to save

### Editing a Movie

1. Find the movie in your collection
2. Tap on the movie card to open details
3. Tap the **Edit** icon
4. Update any information
5. Tap **"Save Changes"**

### Deleting a Movie

1. Find the movie you want to remove
2. Tap on the movie card
3. Tap the **Delete** icon or swipe to delete
4. Confirm the deletion

### Filtering Your Collection

1. On the Movie List page, look for filter options
2. Filter by:
   - **Format** (DVD, Blu-ray, etc.)
   - **Storage Location**
   - **Watched/Unwatched**
   - **Genres**

## Using the Wishlist

The Wishlist helps you keep track of movies you want to watch or purchase in the future.

### Adding to Wishlist

**From Movie Search:**
1. Search for a movie (see [Searching for Movies](#searching-for-movies))
2. Tap the **"Add to Wishlist"** button on the movie card

**Manually:**
1. Navigate to **Wishlist** page from the menu
2. Tap the **"+" (Add)** button
3. Enter movie details:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Release Year**
   - **Priority** (Low, Medium, High)
   - **Reason** (why you want this movie)
   - **Notes**

4. Tap **"Add to Wishlist"**

### Managing Wishlist Items

1. Open the **Wishlist** page from the menu
2. View all your wishlist items
3. Filter by **Priority** (Low, Medium, High)

### Editing Wishlist Items

1. Tap on a wishlist item
2. Edit the details (priority, reason, notes)
3. Save changes

### Moving from Wishlist to Collection

1. Once you acquire a movie from your wishlist
2. Tap on the wishlist item
3. Tap **"Add to Collection"** or **"Mark as Acquired"**
4. The movie will be removed from wishlist (and optionally added to your collection)

### Removing from Wishlist

1. Find the item in your wishlist
2. Swipe to delete or tap the delete icon
3. Confirm removal

## Searching for Movies

The Movie Search feature lets you find movies from online databases and import their information automatically.

### Searching for Movies

1. Open **Movie Search** from the menu
2. Enter a movie title in the search box
3. Results will appear as you type

### Using Search Filters

Refine your search with filters:
- **Minimum Year** - Show movies released after a certain year
- **Maximum Year** - Show movies released before a certain year
- **Minimum Rating** - Show only highly-rated movies

### Importing a Movie

1. Find the movie in search results
2. Tap **"Add to Collection"** to import all details
3. The movie information will be automatically filled in
4. You can edit details before saving
5. Tap **"Save"** to add to your collection

### Adding to Wishlist from Search

1. Find the movie in search results
2. Tap **"Add to Wishlist"**
3. Set priority and add notes if desired
4. Tap **"Save"**

## Viewing Statistics

The Statistics page provides insights into your movie collection.

### Accessing Statistics

1. Navigate to **Summary** or **Statistics** from the menu
2. View your collection overview

### Available Statistics

- **Total Movies** - Total number of movies in your collection
- **Total Wishlist Items** - Number of movies on your wishlist
- **Movies Watched** - How many you've seen
- **Movies Unwatched** - Movies you own but haven't watched yet
- **Total Runtime** - Combined runtime of all movies
- **Movies by Format** - Breakdown of DVD, Blu-ray, Digital, etc.
- **Movies by Genre** - Distribution across genres
- **Movies by Storage Location** - Where your movies are stored
- **Average Personal Rating** - Your average rating across all movies
- **Most Common Genre** - Your most collected genre

### Viewing Charts

- **Pie Charts** - Show format and genre distributions
- **Bar Charts** - Compare different categories

## Tips & Tricks

### 💡 Organize Your Collection

- Use **Storage Location** to track where physical media is kept
- Add **Notes** for special features, condition, or purchase date
- Mark movies as **Watched** to track what you need to see

### 💡 Use the Wishlist Effectively

- Set **Priority** levels to focus on must-have movies
- Add **Reasons** to remember why you wanted each movie
- Check your wishlist before shopping or browsing streaming services

### 💡 Import vs Manual Entry

- Use **Movie Search** to quickly import accurate information
- Manually add older or obscure movies that might not be in databases
- Edit imported movies to add personal details (storage location, notes)

### 💡 Keep Your Collection Updated

- Regularly update **Watched** status
- Add **Personal Ratings** after watching
- Remove movies you no longer own

### 💡 Use Filters

- Filter by **Format** to find specific types of media
- Filter by **Unwatched** to decide what to watch next
- Filter by **Genre** for themed movie nights
